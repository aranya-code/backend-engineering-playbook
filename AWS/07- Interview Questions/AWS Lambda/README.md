# AWS Lambda Interview Guide

> A comprehensive interview preparation guide for **AWS Lambda**, covering fundamentals, architecture, security, performance optimization, scenario-based discussions, and mock interviews for Backend Engineers, Cloud Engineers, and Solution Architects.

---

# Overview

Technical interviews for AWS Lambda have evolved beyond simple service definitions. Modern interviews assess your understanding of **distributed systems**, **serverless architecture**, **production troubleshooting**, **performance optimization**, and **real-world engineering trade-offs**.

This guide is designed to help you prepare for **Senior Backend Developer**, **Software Engineer**, **Cloud Engineer**, **DevOps Engineer**, and **Solution Architect** interviews by combining conceptual knowledge with production-oriented discussions.

Whether you're interviewing at startups or large technology companies, these notes focus on the questions and scenarios commonly encountered during technical interviews.

# Interview Tips

When answering Lambda interview questions:

- Explain concepts instead of memorizing definitions.
- Draw simple architecture diagrams whenever possible.
- Discuss scalability, security, observability, and cost.
- Mention production best practices.
- Explain trade-offs between different approaches.
- Think aloud when solving scenario-based problems.

Senior interviewers are interested in **how you think**, not just **what you know**.

# Key Takeaways

- Senior Lambda interviews focus on architecture, scalability, production systems, and engineering judgment rather than memorization.
- Understanding the reasoning behind architectural decisions is more valuable than simply knowing service features.
- Scenario-based practice helps build confidence for real production discussions.
- Strong communication and structured problem-solving are essential for success in senior backend interviews.
- Mastering these topics prepares you for both technical interviews and real-world serverless application development.
